Your computer, in any browser.

Glass is a free, open-source remote desktop for Mac and Linux: control your computer from an iPad, phone or laptop browser with about 20 ms of delay. Nothing to install on the device in your hands.

$ curl -fsSL glassdesk.dev | sh

Run it on the computer you want to control. macOS 13+ or Linux (beta). Free and open source.

How people use Glass

Most common

Tailscale + Glass: your own computers, from your own devices

Put Glass on the Mac or Linux box you want to reach and Tailscale on everything you own. Your iPad or phone opens it like a website, over your private tailnet: no ports opened, no account, nothing to install on the device in your hand.

1

On the computer

Install Glass

Mac or Linux. glass setup starts it in the background and asks for the two macOS permissions.

curl -fsSL glassdesk.dev | sh
2

On both

Sign in to Tailscale

The same account on the computer and on your device. The free plan is enough. Private and end-to-end encrypted, with no ports opened.

3

On your device

Open the address

In any browser: an iPad, an iPhone or another computer. Your devices are let in automatically; nobody else is.

https://mac-studio.tail4f2a.ts.net

No Tailscale on the device you're holding? glass link --anywhere makes a one-time link that connects directly, end-to-end encrypted. How connecting works
